## The rules

- Each morning: the 20 stocks with the heaviest opening volume against their 14-day average (price over $5, average volume over 1M, daily range over $0.50), from the 500 most-traded stocks the night before.
- Trade the first 5-minute close outside the first 15 minutes' range: above it buys, below it sells short. Entries 9:45 to 3:30. Stop at the middle of the range.
- Risk 0.5% of the morning's equity a trade, at most 25% of equity in one stock, total positions up to 2x equity (margin), at most 8 open. No new trades once the day is down 2%.
- Half comes off at 1R and the stop moves to breakeven. The rest closes at 3:55, unless the trade is past 1R and the stock finished in the top (bottom, for a short) 25% of its day: then it is held overnight with the stop trailed to the day's low (high), for up to 5 days or a 3R target. At most 4 held overnight, worth up to 1x equity.
- Costs: 1 cent a share on every fill, 8% a year margin interest on borrowed money and 3% a year to borrow shorted stock, both charged per night held. Fills are idealized (bar closes and exact stops; a gap through a stop fills at the open).

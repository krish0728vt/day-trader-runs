The rest closes at 3:55, unless the trade is past 1R and the stock finished in the top (bottom, for a short) 25% of its day: then it is held overnight with the stop trailed to the day's low (high), for up to 5 days or a 3R target. At most 4 held overnight, worth up to 1x equity.
- Costs: 1 cent a share on every fill, 8% a year margin interest on borrowed money and 3% a year to borrow shorted stock, both charged per night held. Fills are idealized (bar closes and exact stops; a gap through a stop fills at the open).
